Repiping in Morristown, NJ means replacing the water supply lines in your house with new pipe, either the whole system or the worst sections. It is a planned project, and plumbers walk the house with you before anything is quoted. People usually call when a pipe leaks for the third time, the water runs brown after sitting overnight, or the shower has lost its force.

Not every house needs it. Sometimes one corroded run is the only problem, and fixing that is cheaper than repiping everything. We tell you which case you have, and we give you a clear price before work starts.

Signs your supply pipes are wearing out

Any two of these together make a repipe worth discussing.

  • Water that runs brown or rust-colored when first turned on
  • Low pressure at several fixtures, not just one
  • Repeated pinhole leaks in different spots
  • Flaking, white crusty buildup or green corrosion on exposed pipe

Galvanized steel and old pipe replacement

Older houses around Morristown were often plumbed with galvanized steel, which corrodes from the inside until the opening narrows like a clogged artery. Mid-century copper can also thin out and pinhole, especially at joints.

If your home dates from either era, a quick look at the pipes in the basement ceiling tells us a lot. Galvanized is gray and threaded, copper is orange-brown or green. We check how far the corrosion has progressed before recommending partial or full replacement.

Whole-house or partial repipe

A partial repipe replaces one problem run, such as the line to a bathroom or the kitchen. A whole-house repipe replaces everything from the main shutoff to each fixture.

Partial work makes sense when only one area is failing and the rest tests sound. Whole-house work is better when leaks keep appearing, because patching old pipe often just moves the next failure down the line.

Water service line leaks and replacement

The service line is the buried pipe that brings water from the street to your meter or basement wall. It can leak, corrode or lose pressure long before the pipes inside the house do.

Southeast Morris County Municipal Utilities Authority posts a lead service line inventory online, and it is worth searching your address there. If your service line material is listed as lead or unknown, ask the utility about next steps, and we can replace the customer-side portion as part of a plumbing project. SMCMUA's 2024 water quality report put its lead 90th percentile at 1.3 ppb, under the action level.

Low water pressure

Low pressure has several causes. A partly closed valve, a failed pressure regulator, a clogged aerator or a buried leak can all do it before you need to consider replacing pipe.

We test pressure at the hose bib and at fixtures to separate a whole-house problem from a single bad faucet. If the loss is spread through the house and the pipes are clearly corroded, that points toward repiping. A single fixture may only need faucet repair.

Choosing PEX or copper

PEX is flexible plastic tubing that fishes through walls with fewer cuts, so it usually means less patching. Copper is rigid, familiar and long-lived, and some owners prefer it for exposed runs.

We explain the choice for your house. PEX tends to suit finished homes where opening walls is costly, while copper can make sense in an open basement. Either way, new fittings and a pressure test finish the job.

Planning the work around your household

Most families stay in the house during a repipe, so scheduling matters. We agree on which bathroom stays usable, when the water will be off and where we will stage materials, so the project does not take over your week.

Fixtures that are old or worn are easier to swap while the supply lines are open. If you are adding a bathroom or relocating a sink at the same time, our plumbing installation work can be scheduled into the same visit.

What happens during a repipe

We shut off water, map the existing runs and open small access holes at planned locations. New pipe goes in, then each fixture is reconnected and tested.

Water is off for part of the day, so plan for that. Drywall patches are small, and we describe in advance where they will be. If your house includes an older water heater, it may be sensible to look at water heater replacement while the lines are open.
