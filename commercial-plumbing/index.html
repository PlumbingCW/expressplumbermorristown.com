Flooding, backups and active leaks after hours

Commercial emergencies tend to happen at night and on weekends, because that is when a pipe has been sitting under pressure with nobody looking at it. A split fitting above a drop ceiling can dump water for hours before it shows. A backed-up floor drain in a kitchen can spread to the dining room before the morning shift arrives.

We start by stopping the water and protecting what is dry. Then we find the failure, repair it or make it safe, and tell you what remains for daytime. If the building has a landlord or property manager, we can keep them informed at your direction.

  • Close the main building valve, usually near the meter or in the utility room
  • Cut power to wet outlets and equipment at the panel if you can reach it safely
  • Call property management if shared systems are involved
  • Photograph damage before moving anything

Leak detection for commercial buildings

Commercial leak detection is a limited part of what we do, so here is the honest version. We listen at fixtures and valves, isolate sections of piping, check meters, and use a camera on drains. For leaks buried in walls, under slabs or in large building systems, we are honest if a specialist with tracing equipment is the better choice.

Our advice is simple. If the water meter is moving while every fixture is off, you have a leak. Tell us where the meter is and what it did, and we will say how much we can solve from there. For smaller buildings, our leak detection service covers most cases.

Backflow assembly testing

A backflow preventer keeps dirty water from flowing backward into the drinking supply. Restaurants, medical offices, irrigation systems and fire lines commonly have one. These devices need periodic testing, and a failed test must be repaired and retested.

Requirements differ by property and by utility, so ask your water utility or the town construction office what applies to you and how often. In much of Morristown the utility is the Southeast Morris County Municipal Utilities Authority, and its customer service line is 973-326-6880. We test the assembly, report the result, and repair or replace failed parts.

Grease traps and restaurant drains

A grease trap catches fats and oils before they reach the sewer. When it fills, the kitchen drains slow, odors rise, and the floor drain eventually backs up into the prep area. Traps need regular pumping and inspection, and a trap that overflows after a busy weekend is the classic Monday morning call.

We clear kitchen lines through drain cleaning and jetting, and we check the trap's baffles and connections. Restaurants in older downtown buildings may share older drains. If lines clog repeatedly, a camera inspection tells us whether grease or a damaged pipe is the cause. Heavy buildup is a job for hydro jetting.

Storm drains, roof drains and parking areas

Hurricane Ida's remnants dumped roughly half a foot of rain on the Morristown area in a single day in September 2021, and streets such as Martin Luther King Avenue and Flagler Street closed. A flat roof or parking lot with a clogged drain can pond just as fast.

Before heavy rain, clear leaves and debris from roof drains, scuppers and any catch basins on your property. If water ponds or backs through a floor drain during storms, call us. A repeat backup may trace to the main sewer line. We trace the line, clear it, and tell you whether it needs repair or if the problem sits with the municipal system.
