When a leak means replace, not repair

Some leaks come from a loose fitting, and those are cheap to fix. A leak that appears at the bottom seam or runs from the tank jacket means the inner steel lining has failed, because corrosion has finally cut through the wall.

At that point a new part cannot help. If the heater is also past the age most tanks last, the sensible plan is to retire it. Our water heater repair crews will test first and tell you honestly which side of the line your unit sits on.

What a same-day replacement involves

The plumber drains what is left in the tank, disconnects the water, gas or electric lines and removes the old heater. The new unit goes in on a stable base or drain pan, with fresh flex connectors, a new relief valve and a discharge line routed safely.

Gas models get a venting check and a leak test at every joint. Electric models get their wiring and disconnect inspected before power returns. We then fill, purge air, light or energize the unit and confirm hot water at your fixtures.

Choosing between tank types

A standard gas or electric tank is the quickest swap, because it fits the existing connections. A power-vent or direct-vent gas heater may need a different flue path, which adds time but can improve safety in a tight basement.

If you want endless hot water and are prepared for different piping and venting, read about a tankless conversion before deciding. It is rarely an emergency same-day job, so some homeowners install a tank now and plan the switch later.

Heat pump water heaters as an option

A heat pump water heater pulls warmth from surrounding air to heat the tank instead of burning fuel or running a bare element all day. It uses much less electricity for the same hot water, and it cools and dries the room it sits in, which can be a bonus in a damp basement.

The trade-offs are practical. The unit is taller than many old tanks, needs open air space around it, and needs a condensate drain. Ask about it when you are not in the middle of a flood, since a planned job leaves time to compare options.

Sizing and water quality in Morristown

Morristown water comes from SMCMUA, the regional authority, whose 2024 report showed hardness between 29 and 400 mg/L across its blended sources. Hard water leaves scale on heating surfaces, so test your tap before you pick a unit and ask about a sediment-friendly drain valve. A correctly sized heater that is flushed on schedule will outlast one that is ignored.
