Once the water is stopped, the job becomes a repair question. We find the failed section, cut out the damaged pipe and replace it with the right material for the house. Then we check the rest of the run, because a pipe that failed once is often telling you about its neighbors.

Cracked or broken pipe repair

Pipes split from freezing, corrosion, a stressed joint or a shifting hanger. A hairline crack may weep for weeks before it gives way, while a full burst can empty a gallon every few seconds.

We shut off the section, cut back to sound pipe on both sides and fit new pipe and fittings. Where the damage is limited we repair rather than replace, and we say plainly when the rest of the line looks tired enough to consider repiping.

Copper pipe repair

Copper is the common supply pipe in mid-century houses and it can last a long time, but it pinholes, splits when frozen and fails at poorly made joints. A green crust around a fitting or a spray from a tiny hole means the metal is thin there.

We cut out the bad length and sweat in new copper with fresh fittings, or use approved push-fit connections where a flame is unsafe near framing. Afterward we pressure-test the section before closing anything up.

Frozen pipe repair

Pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages and crawlspaces freeze first. If you open a tap and get a trickle or nothing, a blockage of ice may be sitting in the line and may already have split it.

Leave the faucet open, shut the main if you hear or see water, and never use an open flame on a pipe. We thaw the line safely, check for splits as the ice melts and add insulation where the pipe was exposed. Pipes that froze once usually will again.

Cast iron and drain pipe repair

Older Morristown houses commonly have cast iron drains that rust from the inside, flake and eventually crack or separate at the joints. A wet spot near a stack, a sewage smell or a slow drain with no clog are common signs.

A cracked cast iron section can often be replaced with modern drain pipe using no-hub couplings, without redoing the whole system. If the pipe runs underground to the street, our sewer line repair team can look at it with a camera first.

Leak repair on smaller supply and fixture lines

Not every failure is a gusher. A pinhole in a branch line, a weeping valve or a cracked braided supply under a sink can do real damage if ignored, and these are quick repairs when caught early.

If you cannot tell where a slow leak starts, we may suggest a leak detection visit first, so the repair is aimed at the right spot.
