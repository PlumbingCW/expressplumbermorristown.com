Offices and corporate sites

Corporate buildings bring plumbing that homes do not, including backflow assemblies, floor drains in mechanical rooms and restroom banks served by large lines. A burst riser can flood several floors if nobody is there on a weekend.

Facility managers should keep shutoff locations written down and share them with security staff. We handle after-hours commercial plumbing calls and can coordinate with building engineers on site.

Tenants should know who to call and where the riser valves sit. A short laminated sheet by the mechanical room saves precious time when the water is already moving.

If you can, stay on the phone until the main is off. Meanwhile, clear a path to the shutoff so the technician can start without moving furniture.

The Passaic River edge

The Passaic River forms the borough's eastern boundary. Homes closest to it are the ones to watch when heavy rain stacks up, since saturated ground pushes water toward basements and floor drains.

A pump that runs and a drain that flows are your two best defenses. If either is failing, our sump pump work and a camera look at the lateral can fix the weak link before the next downpour.

After any flood, do not run the washer or dishwasher until the drains are confirmed clear. Extra water into a slow line only makes the backup worse.

Florham, Fairleigh Dickinson and the older estate land

The Florham estate, built by the Twombly family and about 840 acres, is now partly the Fairleigh Dickinson University Florham Campus. Large institutional buildings use plumbing at a scale that makes small leaks costly.

Homes that grew up around the former estate are mixed in age. Older ones may still have cast-iron drains and galvanized supply lines, and a slow drain may mean a sewer line repair is coming.

Who supplies your water

SMCMUA, the regional water authority, lists the Borough of Florham Park among its customers outside its main district. Check your water bill to see who your supplier is, since the answer matters when you need to report a main break.

If the problem is past your meter, it is ours. For taste, hardness or pressure questions on the whole street, ask your water supplier directly rather than relying on a general guess.

If your house is on a well-kept older main, there is nothing to fix. If the bill shows unusual usage, run a meter test and call us before assuming the utility is at fault.

Neighbors beyond the borough line

Florham Park shares edges with several towns, including Madison to the south. We also cover Morris Plains and the rest of the area around Morristown. Whichever side of the line you live on, call the same number.

For kitchen backups, our drain cleaning service is the usual first step, and we can follow with a camera check when the clog returns.
